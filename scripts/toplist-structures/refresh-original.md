## Structure: refresh the original listicle

The source article is OUR OWN existing page. Keep it as the page it already is and bring the
copy up to date. This is a refresh, not a rewrite and not a reframe.

The entries listed in the source article ARE the entries. Keep the same ones, in the same order,
under the same names. Do not add entries, do not drop entries, and do not substitute our catalog
sites for the ones the original names — that would turn a different page into this one.

Build the article in this order:

1. **Intro** (`<p>`, 1–2 short paragraphs)
   - Same premise as the original: what the reader is looking for and what the list covers.
   - Rewrite the sentences so they read as current. Remove anything time-bound that has since
     stopped being true.

2. **One `<h2>` section per entry, in the original's order**
   - `<h2>` headed `1. <Entry Name> — <short tagline>`, incrementing.
   - **Do not put the year in the tagline.** Five headings all ending "in 2026" reads as
     filler and dates the page the moment the year turns. The year belongs in the title only.
   - 1–2 `<p>` describing what it is and who it suits, based on the original's own description
     plus anything the supplied context confirms.
   - Keep the original's actual assessment. If the source called something the best for a given
     purpose, do not silently reverse that; you are refreshing prose, not re-adjudicating.

3. **`<h2>Verdict</h2>`** + one `<p>` — a short closing summary in the original's spirit.

4. **FAQs** (in the `faqs` array, NOT in `content`)
   - 3–5, grounded in what this specific page is about.

## Rules specific to this type

- **No widgets.** No `site-card`, no `site-card-list`, no `pros-cons`. The entries on these
  pages are not sites in our catalog, so there is nothing to embed and no ID to reference.
- **No outbound links to any entry.** Name each one in plain text. Several of these entries are
  sites that compete with us; a mention is acceptable, a link is not — it sends our reader
  somewhere we earn nothing.
- **No prices, percentages or trial terms anywhere in prose.** The original quotes figures that
  expired years ago; that is exactly what must not carry over.
- **Do not year-bump anything you cannot verify.** If the original names a year in a claim, drop
  the year rather than advancing it.
- **Invent nothing.** If the supplied context does not confirm a detail about an entry, describe
  it in the general terms the original used and stop there.
