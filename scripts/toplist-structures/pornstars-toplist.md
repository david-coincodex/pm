## Structure: Pornstar toplist (e.g. "Hottest Brunette Pornstars")

A ranked editorial list of performers. Entries are people, not sites — most are plain text with an auto-inserted photo above each heading. Embed our site widgets only where it genuinely fits (e.g. a network the performer is closely tied to that exists in our catalog). Order:

1. **Intro** (`<p>`, 2 short paragraphs)
   - Set up the angle (e.g. why brunette performers, what makes this list worth reading) for the current year.
   - Briefly explain how the ranking was compiled — cross-referencing multiple expert sources plus our editorial take — without naming raw URLs.

2. **Ranked entries** (`<h2>` `1. <Performer Name>`, up to `maxEntries`)
   For each performer, write 2–4 tight paragraphs / lists covering the "best elements" these sources do well — only what's grounded in the provided context, never invented:
   - **Why she stands out** — looks, on-screen presence, the brunette angle.
   - **Background** — origin/where she's from, when she got into the industry, breakout moment (only if in the sources).
   - **Known for** — signature niches, performance style, notable studios/series/scenes she's associated with.
   - **AVN awards** — if the context lists verified AVN awards for her, work them in naturally with the year (e.g. "named AVN Female Performer of the Year in 2016" or "an AVN Hall of Fame inductee"). Only the awards provided; never invent.
   - Optionally a short `<ul>` of quick highlights ("Best known for…", standout traits, notable work).
   - Do NOT invent ages, measurements, real names, award counts, or dates. Keep specifics only when the sources support them; otherwise stay descriptive.
   - The performer's photo is inserted above the heading automatically — do not add `<img>`.

3. **Source quotes (optional, sparing — MAX 1–2 in the whole article)**
   - Only quote when one of our high-ranked performers is also ranked highly/featured prominently in one of the **external** source listicles; quote that source's praise as a short `<blockquote>` attributed to it.
   - **Never quote pornmode.com — that is our own site.** Attribute only to the other external sources.
   - Don't force it: 0 is fine; never more than 2 per article.

4. **Conclusion / Verdict** (`<h2>` + `<p>`) — wrap-up and the standout #1, plus an honest "who to watch".

5. **FAQs** (returned in the `faqs` field): e.g. "How did we rank these performers?", "What makes a great brunette performer?", topical questions readers search. Grounded, no invented facts.

Tone: confident, vivid, editorial — but factual and non-defamatory. Exactly ~`maxEntries` ranked performers, current year throughout.
